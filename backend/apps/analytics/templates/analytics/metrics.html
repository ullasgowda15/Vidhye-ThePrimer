{% comment %} The internal metrics dashboard (PRODUCT.md §22, §5.6). Staff only — see `apps.analytics.internal`. Deliberately plain: no build step, no JavaScript, no chart library. This page is read by the people building the product, and every dependency it grows is one more thing that can be broken on the morning somebody needs to know what a session costs. Three things it is careful about, all of them honesty rather than style: * every rate shows its **fraction** next to its percentage, so "100%" over two signups cannot be read as a result; * every §22.3 **target** is printed beside the number it judges, with §22.3's own "directional, to be calibrated" caveat at the foot; * cost/session is printed next to **unattributed spend** and the **unpriced span count**, which are the two ways the headline can be understating the truth. Read average DAU, not DAU: one evening's number on an alpha of tens of children is mostly noise about which evening you looked. The average divides by every day in the window, including the days nobody played — dividing by the days that had activity would report a once-a-week product as though its quiet days did not exist. There is no target on stickiness: this is a bedtime product, a low number is not automatically bad news, and §22 has not earned a threshold here yet.

§22.3's targets are directional and to be calibrated on real data — read them as a yardstick, not a grade. Metric definitions, and the judgement calls inside each of them, are written down in apps/analytics/metrics.py; prices are in apps/analytics/pricing.py and are list prices, not quotes.

Product analytics and the parent-facing dashboard are separate by design (§5.6). Nothing on this page is per-child, and nothing here is reachable from the parent API.
