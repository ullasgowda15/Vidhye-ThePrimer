So every section states what its numbers mean and how to read them *next to* the numbers, not in a doc somewhere. The prose is part of the deliverable — a figure whose definition you have to go and look up is a figure that gets misquoted. Three honesty rules it keeps, inherited from `/admin/metrics`: * every rate shows the **fraction** it came from, so "100%" over two signups cannot be read as a result; * every §22.3 **target** is printed beside the number it judges, with §22.3's own "directional, to be calibrated" caveat; * anything that has **not happened yet** reads `·` or `—`, never `0`. How to read this page. The first table is the five metrics the product is judged on. Everything after it exists to explain a number in that table: where families stop, how many children are still here, whether retention is improving, what they play, what it costs, and whether the wellbeing limits are holding. Glossary

Active
A child who started an adventure. Starting counts; finishing is a separate metric.
Cohort
The children whose first finished story fell in the same week. Cohorts are never mixed, which is what lets one be compared with another.
DWA — Delighted Weekly Active
A child who completed three or more adventures in a week. The North Star. A count of children, never a measure of time spent.
Sitting / session
One continuous period of play. It can span more than one adventure, and it is the unit cost is measured per.
Generation / span
One call to a model that cost money — a story beat, an illustration, a line of speech, an embedding. One traced row each.
Unattributed spend
A generation that could not be tied to a sitting. Real money, excluded from cost-per-session, reported separately so the headline is not flattered.
Unpriced generation
A model with no entry in the price table. Counted at zero and flagged, so an unknown price can never be mistaken for a free one.
Matured
A child or a week old enough to be judged. A child whose first story was this morning cannot yet have returned tomorrow, so they are left out of the denominator rather than counted as a miss.

Metric definitions, and the judgement call inside each one, are written down in apps/analytics/metrics.py; prices are list prices from apps/analytics/pricing.py, not quotes. The same numbers print in a terminal with python manage.py metrics --days {{ days }}.

Product analytics and the parent-facing dashboard are separate by design. Nothing on this page is per-child, and nothing here is reachable from the parent API. This sign-in is independent of the Django admin and grants no access to it.
