Three things worth knowing

The profile-field hole was real. avoid_topics, fears and first_name build the safety-policy layer, which is an instruction layer — the kind="data" fence never covered them, so a parent could type into the top of the system prompt. That's now scrubbed at render and again at guard_input.

Writing the red-team corpus found four genuine gaps, all fixed here: a parent typing "spiders" didn't catch "spider"; wanted to die slipped the self-harm rule; "no safety rules" slipped the jailbreak rule; and tag-forgery payloads survived as prose once the brackets were stripped.

What is not proven. The classifier and the vision moderator have no live provider in this environment, so their recall is untested — what's tested is that their absence is recorded as a degradation and never as a pass. Real recall measurement is §19.7's offline eval suite, a later day. The founder manual test in §8 is also still yours to run.
